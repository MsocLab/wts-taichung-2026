IEEE World Technology Summit: Taichung 2026

From Silicon to Physical AI: The Convergence of Semiconductors, HPC and Data Centers

The IEEE World Technology Summits (WTS) connect industry with the IEEE to address the needs of organizations across sectors and regions. The primary focus is on Product and Service Engineering, where real value is created by identifying and addressing common pre-competitive technical challenges faced by engineering and management teams.

Each Summit is shaped by industry leaders, who define the event’s scope, themes, and strategic priorities. The result is a highly relevant, practitioner-driven program centered on real-world challenges, success stories, and actionable insights.

The strength of the WTS lies in its interactive dialogue—a forum for sharing experiences and solutions that benefit both industry professionals and researchers.

Positioning: Industry-led, Results-focused

Moving beyond traditional academic paper presentations, WTS is a strategic platform co-located with IEEE A-SSCC 2026, IEEE Milestone & IEEE Region 10 SYW Event to focus on:

Co-Location

Bringing high-caliber industry executives, chip designers, and academic innovators under one roof.

Pre-competitive Collaboration

A neutral platform for semiconductor and AI leaders to solve shared technical bottlenecks in Physical AI.

International Synergy

Partnering with the IEEE Region 10 SYW to drive cross-border SoC design and hardware–software integration.

AI semiconductor

Chips, memory and process technology that let AI act in the physical world.

HPC and data centers

The compute, interconnect and infrastructure behind training and serving AI at scale.
